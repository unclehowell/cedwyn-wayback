# Survey of marquis of Bute’s Glamorgan estates, by David Stewart. 1824 Survey of marquis of Bute’s Glamorgan estates, by David Stewart. 1824

Document reference Cyfeirnod*    DB/E/1-2
Title Teitl*    Survey of marquis of Bute’s Glamorgan estates, by David Stewart.
Level of description Lefel disgrifiad*    Item
Date Dyddiad*   1824 1824
Description Disgrifiad*    Volume 1 contains plans and reference tables, Volume 2 contains written ‘Terrier’ or ‘Recapitulation’ with annotations, and a general abstract of the estates   
  
DB/E/1 Digital images only.   
(Images on CDs 53-55, GTJ 40108-40202, 64193, 03878-03880)  
  
Farms and lands in: Aberdare, Cardiff (St. John and St. Mary), Cogan, Eglwysilan, Gelligaer, Lavernock, Leckwith, Lisvane, Llanblethian, Llandough-juxta-Penarth, Llanfabon, Llanharan, Llanilid, Llanishen, Llanmaes, Llantrisant, Llantwit Fardre, Llantwit Major, Llanwonno, Merthyr Tydfil, Penderyn, Peterston-super-Ely, Roath, Rudry, St. Andrews Major, St. Brides-super-Ely, St. Fagans, St. Mary Hill, Whitchurch, Ystradyfodwg.  
Reference tables give names of tenants and premises, state of cultivation and acreages. A seperate 'Report and Terrier' gives additional information on rents, leases and state of cultivation.  
Ink and watercolour on paper.  
Scale: 8 chains to 1 inch, unless otherwise stated.  
Dimensions of vol: 22 x 16 ins. [56 x 41 cm.]  
  
CONTENTS:  
1. Cardiff Castle and Grounds, Coopers and Black Friars fields, and Island; in St. John and St. Mary.  
Scale: 3 chains to inch.  
2. Town of Cardiff; in St. John and St. Mary.  
Scale: 3 chains to inch.  
3. House and fields on the Merthyr Road, near Cathays; in St. John.  
Scale: 3 chains to inch.  
4. Cardiff Moors (accomodation fields), Barbers Closes, Dumball, etc.; in St. Mary.  
4a. Land taken by the [Glamorganshire] Canal Co. between town of Cardiff and Sea Lock.  
Scale: 4 chains to inch.  
5. Adamsdown farm, Whitmoor farm, lands on Splott Moors and field at Long Cross; in St. John, St. Mary and Roath.  
6. Cardiff Moors, West of river Taff; in St. Mary.  
7. Place Sturton farms, in St. John.  
7a. Flat Holmes island; in St. Mary.  
Scale: 3 chains to inch.  
8. Deans farm, and land on Pengam Moors; in Roath.  
9. Crwys Bychan, Pen-y-Wain and Skibor Fach farms; in St. John and Roath.  
10. Pen-y-Llan farm; in Roath.  
11. Fair Oak farm and other lands; in Llanishen and Llandaff.  
11a. Merry Hill; in llandaff.  
12. Cefn y Coed and Celin farms; in Llanishen.  
13. Rhyd y Penna, Rhyd y Blewin and other lands; in Llanishen.  
14. Hewl Hir farm, in Llanishen; Tyr Sion Gruffydd farm and cottage and garden; in Lisvane.  
15. Tyr y Fillog, in Whitchurch; cottages and lands in Whitchurch and Llandaff.  
Scale: 4 chains to inch.  
Inset: Map of land near canal. Scale: 2 chains to inch.  
16. Lequeth Moors; in Leckwith.  
17. Lequeth Bridge Farm, Lower Ynis Ton farm, Lequeth Church farm, Hill farm, Well Spring and other properties; in Leckwith.  
18. Farm Wen; in Leckwith.  
19. Cedfin alias Ty Mawr farm; in Llandough.  
20. Llandough and Cogan Pill farms, and other lands; in Llandough.  
21. Corners Well farm, Green Seys farm and other lands; in Cogan, Llandough and St. Andrews Major.  
22. Cogan farm, and other lands; in Cogan.  
23. Cosmeston and Swanbridge farms; in Lavernock.  
24. Cournix farm; in Llancarfan.  
25. Walterstone farm; in Llancarfan.  
26. Picketsone farm and various tenements and lands; in Llanmaes.  
27. Batts Lays farm; in Llantwit Major.  
28. Lechmoor lands, Crooks Ground and other lands; in Llantwit Major.  
29. Sheep Lays farm; in Llantwit Major.  
29a. Cottages and lands; in Llanblethian.  
Scale: 4 chains to inch.  
29b. Encroachments; in Llantwit Major.  
Scale: 6 chains to inch.  
30. Cottages at Aburthin; in Llanblethian.  
Scale: 4 chains to inch.  
31. Lands at Trallwin, Coed Shon Bach, Llwyn y Peel and other llands; in Llanilid ans St. Mary Hill.  
32. Ynis y Mairdy, Garth Maelog and Tyr Christopher y Gof; in Llanharan.  
33. Cilvach Ithill and other lands; in Llantrisant.  
34. Abergorgay and Ystrad y Fyrchan, including also Gynmoch, Tir Gibondio, Pentwyn, Pandy, Tulady and Glyn Coly; in Ystradyfodwg.  
Inset: Map of Pandy public house. Scale: 3 chains to inch.  
35. Part of Rhigos Estate, comprising: Gwrelych mill, Hendra Fawr farm, Rhyd farm, part of Bayly Glace, Blaen Gwrelych farm, Blaen Gwrangon farm, Gwrangon Genol farm, Gwrangon Fawr farm, Cenol, and cottages at Pentwyn alias Cwm Hwnt; in Ystradyfodwg.  
36. Part of Rhygoes Estate; in Ystradyfodwg.  
37. Part of Rhygoes Estate, comprising: Llwyn y Gwyn, Ynis y Cambool and Sych Pont, Nant Gwina farm, Pen y Cae Drain farm, Carn y gist farm and Werva Ycha farm; in Ystradyfodwg.  
38. Part of Rhygoes Estate, comprising: Tyr y Castall farm, Cae Ton Rees, Nant y Llecha farm, Brin y Gare farm and Ty Newydd farm; in Ystradyfodwg.  
39. Part of Rhygoes Estate, including Hirwain Iron Works, Brin y Mairdy, Brin y Cnappa and Tyr Evan Gof; in Ystradyfodwg, Penderin and Aberdare.  
40. Building ground at Hirwain.  
Scale: 4 chains to 1 inch.  
41. Scattered lands adjoining Hirwain Common; in Aberdare.  
Scale: 4 chains to 1 inch.  
42. Cwm Dare Fuchan and Tyr Vryd alias Rhw Menich; in Aberdare.  
--. Building ground at Aberdare.  
Scale: 4 chains to 1 inch.  
43. Sundry farms leased to the Aberdare Iron Co., including Pentra Bach alias Tyr y Coll and Tir y Pimp Pint, Tyr y Gwrid alias Tyr Draw, Tyr y Gwrid Yshaf, Graig y Gilvach, Pant y Gardinian, and Cwm Bach farm; in Aberdare.  
44. Forch Don; in Llanwonno.  
45. Clun y Felin; in Llanwonno.  
46. Ynis y Wern farm, Cae Darran, Tyr Pen y Graig farm and Cae Glyn Taff; in Llantwit Fardre.  
47. Maes Bach farm, and other lands; in Llantwit Fardre.  
48. Bryn yr Eglwys and Tyr Poth y Dowlias farm; in Llantwit Fardre.  
49. Cae'r Dyon, Coed Cae Arlwydd and Disgwlfa; in Llantwit Fardre.  
50. 'Lantrissent' castle etc.; in Llantrisant.  
Scale: 2 chains to 1 inch.  
51. Cloddia Cabban farm, Llwd Cae Mawr farm, Gwaine Miskin, Coed Cae Cenol and Gwaine y Stent; in Llantrisant.  
52. Caira Bach, Caira Hill, Gwern y Mole farm and Cae Tri Burlin; in Llantrisant.  
53. New Park, Hendry, Maes Harrill, Ponty Park, Fenalog, The Farm and other lands; in Llantrisant.  
54. Goced, Cwm y Fwch, Llwyn yr Es, Cae Rhyw farm, and other lands; in Pentyrch.  
55. Park Coed Marchan and Henstaff farms; in St. Brides[-super-Ely], Peterston-super-Ely and St. Fagans.  
56. Cottages on the Garth; in Pentyrch.  
Scale: 2 chains to 1 inch.  
57. Blaen Morlais and Cottages at Pant Cad Ifor; in Merthyr Tydfil.  
58. Dowlais furnace ground; in Merthyr Tydfil.  
Scale: 4 chains to 1 inch.  
59. Marchandy Wain alias Twyn y Wain, Isha Wain, Bryn y Caera and Bryn Keath; in Merthyr Tydfil and Gelligaer.  
60. Blaen Rumney farm, Lower Blaen Rumney farm, Carno, Blaen Carno, Pidwelt farm and other lands; in Gelligaer.  
61. Havod Jestyn alias Troed y Rhyw; in Gelligaer.  
62. Lands at Ysgwydd Gwin; in Gelliager.  
63. Tyr Verch Gronow and Cae Canol; in Gelligaer.  
64. Tyr y Colly farm; in Gelligaer.  
Insets: Plans of Cae Glas, Gelligaer pound and Llwyd y Capel. Scale: 5 chains to 1 inch.  
65. Cwm Gillia alias Melindra Gillia, Bryn Owen and Tyr William Thomas; in Gelligaer and Llanfabon.  
66. Caerphilly Castle; in Eglwysilan.  
Scale: 4 chains to 1 inch.  
67. Cefn y Coed, Coed Cae Rosser and Cae Tri Dinas; in Llanfabon and Eglwysilan.  
67a. Cottages, including Pwll y Pant, Pont y Gwindy, Caermarchog, The Angel public house and Quarra Mawr; in Eglwysilan.  
Scale: 4 chains to 1 inch.  
68. Ty yn y Wern and Hewl y Bwnshy farms; in Eglwysilan.  
69. Forest Keven y Veed alias Castle Coch Forest, and adjoining lands including Cwm Novidd; in Eglwysilan and Whitchurch.  
70. Cottages and lands; in Eglwysilan and Whitchurch.  
Scale: 4 chains to 1 inch.  
71. Forest Isha farm alias Oakingham Forest; in Whithurch.  
Scale: 6 chains to 1 inch.  
Inset: Plan of cottage at Hewl Don. Scale: 5 chains to 1 inch.  
72. Cottages and land at Whitchurch.  
Scale: 4 chains to 1 inch.  
73. Coed y Gwina alias Cefn Poth; in Michaelston y Fedw.  
74. Caerphilly Common, cottages and lands; in Eglwysilan,  
Scale: 6 chains to 1 inch.  
75. Rudry Common, cottages and lands; in Rudry.  
Inset: Map of lands near Maen Llwyd.
Extent Maint*    2 large volumes
Related material Deunydd perthnasol*    See 'Glamorgan Estate Maps' by Hilary Thomas for full description of the many areas covered by these plans
